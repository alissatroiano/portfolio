Logic Lift: Legacy Mainframe to Modern Logic

Honorable Mention - Gemini 3 DeepMind Hackathon, 04/2026

About

Logic Lift is an autonomous modernization platform that deconstructs monolithic COBOL systems and reconstructs them into future-proof, cloud-native Python.

Problem

While navigating the job market, I noticed a surprising surge in advertisements for COBOL—a language created in 1959. Over 95% of ATM swipes and 43% of banking systems still run on these legacy mainframes, yet the experts who maintain them are retiring.

Solution

Logic Lift identifies buried business rules and PIC-clause data mappings, generates parity test suites (pytest) to prove new logic behaves identically, aggregates processed modules into a deployable queue file, and maps the legacy codebase topology into a modern distributed architecture.

Tech Stack

Google AI Studio, TypeScript, React, Gemini 3
